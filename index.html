TimberWatch Privacy Policy

Effective: November 2, 2026

Information stored on your device

TimberWatch stores the inventory records, mill settings, photos, labels, kiln records, zones, backup preferences, and legal-acceptance record that you enter or create on your Android device. The beta does not require a TimberWatch account. The app also generates a random identifier for your installation. It is stored with your legal-acceptance record and included in backup files so records can be matched to the device that created them. It is not linked to your name, phone number, or any advertising identifier.

Network and connected features

TimberWatch has no cloud sync and does not run a server that receives your data. TimberWatch only sends information off your device when you deliberately use a connected feature such as printing, sharing, exporting, or saving a backup to a destination you choose. The receiving device, app, service, or storage provider handles that information under its own terms.

Permissions

Camera access is used only when you scan a barcode or capture supported content. Bluetooth and local-network access are used to connect to printers or equipment you select. Notification access is optional and is used for backup and YardHawk alerts.

Analytics and advertising

This beta does not include advertising, behavioral tracking, or TimberWatch analytics collection. TimberWatch's own diagnostic logs remain on the device unless you choose to share them.

Google ML Kit (scanner)

The barcode and text scanner uses Google ML Kit, which runs on your device. Google's ML Kit software sends Google limited diagnostic and usage information about the scanner, such as device model, Android version, app version, scanner performance and error codes, along with an installation identifier that is not intended to identify you. It does not send camera images, scanned codes or inventory data. Google uses this information to maintain and improve ML Kit, as described in Google's ML Kit data disclosure.

Retention and deletion

App data remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Exported files and backups remain wherever you saved or shared them and must be deleted from those destinations separately.

Security

TimberWatch relies on Android device protections and blocks cleartext (unencrypted HTTP) network traffic. You are responsible for protecting your device, printer, backup locations, and exported files from unauthorized access.

Children and changes

TimberWatch is a business tool and is not directed to children. This policy may be updated when connected features or data practices change.
